The short versionWe do not ask you to create an account, we do not run advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not sell personal information.

The weekly checklist saves on your device only. If you email us, we receive your email and whatever you include in it. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs that include technical data such as your IP address. That is essentially the whole story.

1. Who we are

GardenWow (“GardenWow”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is an independent city-gardening guide published at gardenwow.co. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, GardenWow is the controller of the limited personal information described in this policy.

You can reach us at hello@gardenwow.co. We are a small independent publication, not a large data-driven company. That informs how we treat your information: we collect as little as possible, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

We do not currently offer accounts, comments, or a newsletter signup form. If you contact us by email — for example with a question, a correction, or a photograph — we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and any other information you choose to send. Please do not send sensitive personal information, and do not send photographs containing identifiable people unless you have their permission.

Technical information collected automatically

When you visit the website, our hosting provider may process standard technical information in order to serve pages and keep the site secure. This can include:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • your browser type, device type, operating system and screen size;
  • the pages you request, the date and time of your request, and the referring page;
  • error and security logs, including suspicious request patterns.

We use this information to operate and secure the site, diagnose problems and understand aggregate traffic levels. We do not use it to identify individual visitors or to serve advertising.

Information stored on your device

If you use the 15-minute weekly reset checklist, your checked tasks are stored in your browser’s local storage so that they are still there when you return. This information stays on your device; it is not transmitted to us. You can clear it by using the reset button or by clearing site data in your browser.

We do not knowingly collect any other information from you, and we do not combine information from different sources to build a profile.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, tracking pixels or third-party embedded content. Our fonts and images are served from our own site, so loading a page does not send your data to a third-party font or image network.

The checklist uses browser local storage, not a cookie. It stores only the identifiers of the tasks you have checked, under the key gardenwow:weekly-reset:v1. It is functional storage, not tracking, and it is not shared with anyone.

Some hosting providers set strictly necessary security cookies or similar technologies to protect the site from abuse. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent before setting them.

You can delete local storage at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will reset your checklist progress; it will not affect any other part of the site.

4. How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • deliver the website and its content to you;
  • keep the site secure, prevent abuse and fix technical problems;
  • respond to your emails, questions and requests;
  • understand aggregate, non-identifying traffic levels and improve the guide;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use your email address to send marketing messages unless you have specifically asked us to.

5. Our legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the EU or UK GDPR apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests: operating, securing and improving the website, and responding to messages you send us. We keep this processing proportionate and limited.
  • Consent: where we ask for it — for example, if we introduce optional analytics or a newsletter. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

Where India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, we process personal data on the basis of your consent or another lawful ground recognised under that law, and we honour your rights under it.

6. When we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers. A hosting provider, email provider or similar supplier may process information on our behalf so we can run the site and answer emails. They are expected to protect it and use it only for the service they provide.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order or a valid legal request, or if necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of GardenWow, our readers or others.
  • Business changes. If the publication is transferred, merged or reorganised, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards and this policy.

We do not share information with advertising networks, data brokers or social media platforms for their own marketing.

7. International transfers

Our hosting and email providers may process information in countries other than the one you live in. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers, such as standard contractual clauses or an equivalent lawful mechanism. Because the information we handle is limited, the practical risk is low — but we do not treat that as an excuse to be careless.

8. How long we keep it

We keep emails you send us for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of the conversation, then for any additional period required by law. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider for a short operational period, typically no more than a few weeks, unless a security incident requires longer retention.

Checklist data remains in your browser until you reset it or clear your browser data. We do not have a copy of it to delete.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete information, subject to legal exceptions;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • request portability of information you provided to us;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email hello@gardenwow.co. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request relates to you, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a limitation right. You may still contact us with any request or question.

If you are in the EEA or UK: you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

10. Children

GardenWow is a general-audience gardening guide and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 18 where Indian law requires parental consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited information we handle, including encrypted connections where the site is served over HTTPS and access controls on our accounts. No website or email system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information by email.

If we become aware of a security incident that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify affected people and authorities where required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we change how the site works or when the law changes. The “last updated” date at the top will always show the current version. If a change is material, we will make it clear on the site. Continued use of GardenWow after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact us

Questions, requests or concerns about privacy can go to hello@gardenwow.co. We are a small team, so please allow a reasonable time for a reply — but we do read everything.

If you are not satisfied with our response and you are in the EEA or UK, you can contact your local supervisory authority. If you are in India, you can contact the Data Protection Board of India once the relevant mechanisms are operational.
